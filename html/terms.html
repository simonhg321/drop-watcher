1. Program Description

Drop Watcher (instockornot.club) is a free web monitoring service that sends SMS and email alerts when specific keywords appear on webpages you are tracking. SMS messages are transactional alerts only — never marketing, promotions, or advertising.

When you opt in to SMS, you will receive text message alerts when keywords you specified are detected on a webpage you are watching.

2. How You Opt In (Call to Action)

Drop Watcher uses a single opt-in method: a web signup form at instockornot.club/watchlist.html.

Web Form Opt-In Flow
1.
User visits instockornot.club and fills out the watchlist signup form with their name, email, and the URL + keywords they want to monitor.
2.
User optionally enters their phone number in the SMS field.
3.
User checks an explicit, unchecked-by-default SMS consent checkbox. The checkbox is required to enable SMS — the form does not proceed with SMS without it.
4.
The following consent language is displayed directly below the phone number field and checkbox at the point of collection:
"By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS alerts from Drop Watcher (instockornot.club) about knife and EDC gear availability. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help."

SMS opt-in is entirely optional. Email alerts are available without providing a phone number. Users are not required to opt in to SMS as a condition of using the service.

Note on page access: The signup form is personalized per watcher (each user's watchlist dashboard is private). The opt-in consent flow described above and the consent language shown are consistent for all users at signup. Screenshots of the opt-in flow are available upon request at support@instockornot.club.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on keyword matches on your watched pages. You may receive anywhere from zero to several messages per day depending on activity. Typical usage is a few messages per week. Drop Watcher only sends alerts when a monitored keyword is actually detected — there are no scheduled or periodic messages.

Msg & data rates may apply. Check with your carrier for details on your messaging plan.

4. Opt-Out Instructions

You can stop receiving SMS alerts at any time by any of these methods:

  • Reply STOP to any message from Drop Watcher
  • Using the STOP button on your personal dashboard at instockornot.club
  • Contacting support@instockornot.club

Opt-out keywords recognized:

STOP STOPALL UNSUBSCRIBE CANCEL END QUIT REVOKE

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message. You will not receive any further SMS unless you opt in again.

6. Privacy & Data Use

Your phone number and SMS opt-in consent are not sold, rented, or shared with any third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Your phone number is shared only with our SMS delivery provider (Twilio) for the sole purpose of delivering your alerts.

For full details on how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
