One page. One keyword. One alert.

You give us a page and a word. We go look at that page for you, every 15 minutes or so, and the moment the word shows up we send you an email or a text. That's the whole thing. We only look at the pages people ask us to. There is no crawler behind this and no feed to browse. It's a tripwire, not a search engine.

THREE STEPS

01

Paste the page

The exact page where the thing will appear. A dealer's Chris Reeve collection, a maker's "available" page, one product listing. The closer the page is to the thing, the better it works. A homepage is usually too far away.

02

Type the word

What you're waiting to see on that page. A model name is best. "In stock" or "add to cart" works too, but it fires for everything on the page, so you'll hear from us a lot.

03

Tell us where to reach you

Email, or a phone number for a text. We confirm it once, then leave you alone until the word shows up. Every alert has a one-click link to stop.

WHAT IT WON'T DO

It won't buy anything. It watches and it tells you. You do the rest.
It won't crawl a store. It reads the one page you gave it and nothing past it.
It won't watch a page that needs a login or one that only draws itself with JavaScript. We'll tell you when you paste the URL.
It isn't a deal finder for everything. It was built for knives and EDC gear. Point it at a big-box retailer and we'll ask if you're sure. If you are, it's your call.
